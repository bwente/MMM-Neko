# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to participate in MMM-Neko, regardless of background,
identity, or experience. Treat others with respect in issues, pull requests,
and other project discussions.

- Be considerate and patient. Focus criticism on the work, not the person.
- Respect different perspectives and accept constructive feedback.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or use sexualized language or imagery.
- Do not publish someone else's private information without permission.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and avoid spam.

Report concerns privately to Brian Wente through a direct message to
[bwente on the MagicMirror forum](https://forum.magicmirror.builders/user/bwente).
Include links and enough context to understand what happened; avoid posting
sensitive details in a public issue.

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, close or lock discussions,
and restrict participation when needed to keep the community welcoming.
Reports will be handled with respect for the privacy of those involved.
